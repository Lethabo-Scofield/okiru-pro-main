# Okiru Demo Script — "Upload your documents, get a B-BBEE score"

**Demo client:** Kagiso Facilities Services (Pty) Ltd (fictional) · RCOGP · QSE
**Runtime:** ~4–5 minutes · **Goal:** show that messy documents → an audit-grade scorecard in minutes.

> How to use this: the **left column is what you SAY** (read it naturally, don't
> memorise). The right notes are **what you DO / what to point at**. Pause where
> you see ⏸. If you lose your place, just narrate what's on screen — the flow
> carries itself.

---

## 0. Before you hit record (setup — 30 sec, off-camera)
- Sign in to Okiru. Land on the **Hub**.
- Have the demo folder open in a file window: `Demo - Kagiso Facilities Services (RCOGP)`.
- Close other tabs. Zoom the browser to ~110% so text is readable on video.

---

## 1. The hook (0:00–0:30)

> "Getting a B-BBEE scorecard usually means weeks of back-and-forth with a
> consultant, and a spreadsheet nobody understands. I'm going to show you how
> Okiru turns a folder of your existing documents into an audit-grade scorecard
> in about five minutes. No forms to fill in — you just upload what you already
> have."

**DO:** Click **Get started → Create Scorecard.**

---

## 2. Upload the documents (0:30–1:30)

> "This is Kagiso Facilities Services — a mid-sized cleaning and facilities
> company. Here's their evidence: their B-BBEE information workbook, their
> company profile, and their financials. I'll just drag the whole folder in."

**DO:** Drag the files from the demo folder onto the **upload area**. ⏸ Let them land.

> "Okiru reads each document — the share register, the payroll, the supplier
> schedule, the training records — and pulls the numbers out automatically. You
> can see it working through them here."

**POINT AT:** the file list appearing, each with a **token count** and a **status**
(Quoted / Review). ⏸ Wait for processing to settle.

> "It tells me honestly what it read cleanly and what it wants me to glance at —
> it's not pretending. Anything it's unsure about, I can still continue and fill
> in later. Nothing blocks me."

---

## 3. Set the company profile (1:30–2:00)

> "On the right I just confirm two things: the sector and the size."

**DO:** In **Company Profile**, set **Sector = (RCOGP / General)** and
**Organisation size = QSE**. ⏸

> "Kagiso turns over about R24 million a year, so they're a Qualifying Small
> Enterprise. That's it — no other setup."

**DO:** Click **Continue.**

---

## 4. The indicative score (2:00–3:15) — the "wow"

> "And there's the scorecard."

**POINT AT the top row:**
> "Total score, their B-BBEE level, their procurement recognition, and — this is
> new — the **total Rand value we read off their documents**, so they can sanity-
> check that we captured everything."

**POINT AT the pillar bars, scroll slowly:**
> "Every element is here — Ownership, Management Control, Employment Equity,
> Skills, Procurement, and so on — each scored against the Codes."

**POINT AT a coverage note (the ⓘ line under a pillar):**
> "And where the Codes are subtle, it says so in plain English. For example here
> it explains exactly why a bonus did or didn't apply — the kind of thing that
> usually only a verification analyst would catch. It's honest about the score,
> not just a number."

**POINT AT "We reconciled your documents into one company profile":**
> "It also merged everything into one clean profile — 100% black-owned, the
> supplier count, and so on — from documents that didn't agree with each other."

---

## 5. Refine in the workbook (3:15–4:15)

> "If I want to go deeper, I open the full workbook."

**DO:** Click **Open workbook to refine.** ⏸

> "This is the complete scorecard. Every pillar expands to show the exact
> indicators and how each one was calculated — the same breakdown an auditor
> works from. If a number's wrong or a document was missing, I edit it here and
> the score updates live."

**DO:** Expand one pillar (e.g. **Ownership** or **Procurement**) to show the
sub-lines. ⏸

> "So in a few minutes we went from a folder of documents to a defensible,
> audit-ready B-BBEE scorecard — with the working shown."

---

## 6. Close (4:15–4:45)

> "That's Okiru. Upload what you already have, and get a scorecard you can stand
> behind — for a fraction of the time and cost. If you'd like to try it with your
> own documents, [your call-to-action here]."

**DO:** Stop recording.

---

## Flow cheat-sheet (tape this next to your screen)

1. **Get started → Create Scorecard**
2. **Drag the folder in** → let it process → "it reads it honestly"
3. **Sector + Size (QSE)** → **Continue**
4. **Top row:** score · level · recognition · **money read**
5. **Scroll pillars** → point at a **coverage note**
6. **Open workbook to refine** → expand one pillar
7. **Close**

## If something goes wrong on camera
- A file shows **Review** with a warning → that's fine, say *"it flags what to double-check, I can still continue"* and move on. It's a feature, not an error.
- Score looks different than expected → say *"the score reflects exactly what's in the documents — if I add more evidence it moves"*. Never apologise for the number; it's honest.
- Processing is slow → keep talking about what the documents are while it works.
