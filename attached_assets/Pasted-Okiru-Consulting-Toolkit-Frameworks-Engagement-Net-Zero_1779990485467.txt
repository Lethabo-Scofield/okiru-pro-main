02
The Challenge
Why most organisations
can't get to Net Zero — and what Okiru solves.
Three structural gaps stand between South African organisations and credible, audit-grade transformation reporting.

The Measurement Gap
Scope 3 remains uncaptured
70–90%
of emissions sit in the supply chain
Most organisations measure Scope 1 confidently, Scope 2 with effort, and Scope 3 with estimates. The majority of supply-chain emissions are the hardest to measure — and the largest opportunity to reduce.

The Reporting Gap
Frameworks are tightening
IFRS S2
CDP & SBTi are raising the bar
IFRS S1 and S2 are mandatory in many jurisdictions. CDP penalises spend-based methodologies. SBTi requires activity-based targets. The bar for credible reporting rises every cycle.

The Execution Gap
Data lives in the wrong places
5 silos
→ one annual report
Energy is in finance. Waste is in operations. Workforce is in HR. CSI is in marketing. Until these sources speak to each other, the ESG report is reconstructed from scratch every year.
